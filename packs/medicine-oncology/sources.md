# Sources: medicine-oncology (Ung thư học) 0.1.0 draft

Mined 2026-10-15 (run `pack-2026-10-15-medicine-oncology`). Counts are numbers of **documents** of the shared corpus
(D:/vitranslate-corpus, 291 Vietnamese full texts at counting time) that contain the Vietnamese form as a whole word,
after NFC and old-style tone folding (hoá = hóa, tuỷ = tủy). Region of a document by host: S = South (Tạp chí Y học
TP.HCM, Y Dược học Cần Thơ, BV Bình Dân, BV Nguyễn Tri Phương, BV ĐH Y Dược TP.HCM, Phạm Ngọc Thạch), H = Huế, N = North /
national (TCNCYH, YDLS 108, VJRNM, BV 103, BVĐK Thái Nguyên...), B = Ministry of Health texts.

About 30 of the 291 documents are clinical-oncology texts: the largest Southern one is the BV Bình Dân oncology
protocol (Phác đồ Ung bướu 2020, already in the corpus); 28 oncology articles were added for this pack (7 Y học
TP.HCM, 4 Cần Thơ, 4 Huế, 10 TCNCYH, 3 YDLS 108). Caveats:
- one long protocol counts as one document, however many times it uses a form;
- a short form is also counted inside longer ones ("củng cố" inside "điều trị củng cố");
- "counted form" in a note means the count is of a related form (often the Northern / family form), not of the
  chosen value itself; rows "no count" follow a family rule or a guideline form and are listed in MINING_REPORT.md;
- web search results (titles and abstracts seen in the search tool, no download) are cited by journal name only.

No sentence of any paper is copied here: only term pairs, counts and the URL of up to 3 documents that contain the
counted form.
