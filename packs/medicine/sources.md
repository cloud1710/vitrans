# Sources: medicine (Nội khoa) 0.1.0 draft

Mined 2026-10-15 (run `pack-2026-10-15-medicine`). Counts are numbers of **documents** of the shared corpus
(D:/vitranslate-corpus, 253 Vietnamese full texts at counting time: journal PDFs plus a few hospital and Ministry of
Health documents) that contain the Vietnamese form as a whole word, after NFC and old-style tone folding
(hoá = hóa, tuỷ = tủy). Region of a document by host: S = South (Tạp chí Y học TP.HCM, Y Dược học Cần Thơ, BV Nguyễn Tri
Phương, BV ĐH Y Dược TP.HCM, BV Bình Dân, Phạm Ngọc Thạch, HOSREM), H = Huế, N = North / national journals
(TCNCYH, YDLS 108, VJS, VJRNM, VJOG...), B = Ministry of Health texts (Hướng dẫn quốc gia về Cảnh giác dược 2015,
QĐ 2111/QĐ-BYT, copy on who-umc.org; QĐ 5481/QĐ-BYT 2020 đái tháo đường típ 2, copy on benhviendakhoabaria.vn).

Caveats:
- the corpus was built by several miners (kidney, thyroid, molecular, nutrition, pathology, obstetrics, radiology), so
  general clinical words are counted over mixed specialties; 21 internal-medicine / geriatrics / pharmacovigilance /
  diabetes documents were added for this pack;
- a short form is also counted inside longer ones ("chỉnh liều" inside "điều chỉnh liều"); the choice uses the
  longer form when that is the case;
- counts below 3 documents are marked LOW in the glossary note; rows with "no corpus count" are textbook / guideline
  forms chosen without a count (listed in MINING_REPORT.md).

No sentence of any paper is copied here: only term pairs, counts and the URL of up to 3 documents that contain the
counted form.
